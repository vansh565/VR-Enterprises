Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information VR Enterprises (“we”, “us”) receives through www.vrenterprises.in and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you use our product enquiry, distributor or contact forms, you share details such as your name, mobile number, email address, city and state, business name and type, product and quantity of interest, and your message.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, provide quotations, process orders and discuss distribution. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How form submissions are delivered

Our forms are delivered to our business email by Web3Forms, a form-processing service. Your submission passes through their servers to reach us.

WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, the message is sent through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Messages pre-filled by our website are only sent when you press send in WhatsApp.

Third-party services on this website

  • Google Maps — the embedded map on our contact section is provided by Google and may set its own cookies.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) — supply fonts and icons; your browser connects to them to load these files.

Cookies and storage

Our website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. To prevent the same enquiry being sent twice, your browser temporarily remembers a fingerprint of a sent form (not its content) in session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.

Keeping and deleting your information

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed for business and legal purposes. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have shared with us by contacting us below.
