Our Manufacturing Process

Six Careful Steps, No Shortcuts

Every bottle of VR cold pressed black mustard oil goes through the same simple, traditional process.

  1. Seeds
  2. Cleaning
  3. Cold press
  4. Filtration
  5. Inspection
  6. Pure oil
  1. 01

    Black Mustard Seed Selection

    We choose mature, well-dried black mustard seeds, checking each lot for size, colour and foreign matter before it is accepted.

  2. 02

    Seed Cleaning

    Seeds are mechanically cleaned and sieved to remove dust, stones and husk — no chemical washing.

  3. 03

    Traditional Cold Pressing

    The seeds are pressed kachi ghani style, without external heat, so the oil keeps its natural colour, pungency and aroma.

  4. 04

    Oil Filtration

    The pressed oil is allowed to settle and is filtered to remove seed particles, leaving a clear, golden oil.

  5. 05

    Quality Inspection

    Each batch is checked for clarity, colour, aroma and fill before release. Batch reports are published below as they become available.

  6. 06

    Hygienic Packaging

    Oil is filled into clean, food-grade bottles and jars, sealed, and labelled with batch number, manufacturing date and best-before information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good Questions

What is cold pressed mustard oil?

Cold pressed mustard oil is extracted by mechanically pressing mustard seeds without applying external heat. In India this traditional first pressing is called kachi ghani. The oil keeps the natural golden colour, strong aroma and pungency of the seed.

What makes black mustard oil different?

Black mustard seeds give an oil with a sharper, more pungent aroma than many yellow-seed oils. That bold flavour is why it is preferred for pickles, tadka and traditional North Indian, Kashmiri and Bengali cooking.

Is anything added to VR mustard oil?

No. The only ingredient is cold pressed black mustard oil. We do not blend it with other oils or add preservatives, colours or flavours.

What packaging sizes are available?

200 ml, 500 ml, 1 Litre and 2 Litre bottles, a 5 Litre jar, and a carton of 5 × 1 Litre bottles. See the product range for details.

How should mustard oil be stored?

Keep the bottle tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat from the stove. Use it within the best-before period printed on the label. Cold pressed oil may turn slightly cloudy in very cold weather — this is natural and clears at room temperature.

Where do I find the batch number and dates?

The batch number, manufacturing date and best-before information are printed on the label of every pack. You can enter the batch number in Trace Your Batch to see its published record.
