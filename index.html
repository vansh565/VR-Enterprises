Our Manufacturing Process

The Cold-Press Timeline

Six careful steps from seed selection to sealed packaging — no heat, no shortcuts, no additives.

01

Seed Selection

Premium black mustard seeds are hand-sourced from trusted farms and inspected for quality and maturity.

02

Cleaning

Seeds are mechanically cleaned and sieved to remove dust, stones and impurities — no chemical wash.

03

Cold Pressing

Seeds are pressed at low temperature using a traditional wooden-press (kachi ghani) to retain natural nutrients and aroma.

04

Filtration

The extracted oil passes through natural filtration to remove residual particles while keeping the oil pure.

05

Quality Check

Each batch is lab-tested for purity, moisture and adulteration before being approved for packaging.

06

Packaging

Approved oil is hygienically sealed in food-grade bottles and cans, labelled with batch and date codes.

Recipe Preserving

Homemade Mango Pickle (Achar)

Step-by-step guide to making long-lasting mango pickle using cold pressed mustard oil as the base.

Preparation Time

45 minutes + 3 days resting

Servings

Makes 1 large jar

Ingredients

  • 1 kg raw mangoes, cut into pieces
  • 200 ml VR Enterprises Cold Pressed Mustard Oil
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp fenugreek seeds (methi)
  • 1 tbsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry mango pieces thoroughly (no water should remain).
  2. Heat VR Enterprises Cold Pressed Mustard Oil in a pan until smoking point, then cool slightly.
  3. In a bowl, mix mango pieces with salt, chili powder, and turmeric.
  4. Dry roast fenugreek seeds, mustard seeds, and fennel seeds, then grind to a coarse powder.
  5. Add the spice powder and asafoetida to the mango mixture, mix well.
  6. Pour the cooled mustard oil over the mixture and add lemon juice.
  7. Mix everything thoroughly and transfer to a clean, dry glass jar.
  8. Place in sunlight for 2-3 days, shaking the jar daily.
  9. The pickle is ready when the mango softens and oil floats on top.
  10. Store in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.

Pro Tips

  • Make sure all utensils and the jar are completely dry to prevent spoilage.
  • Use raw, firm mangoes for the best texture.
  • The oil should completely cover the mango pieces for preservation.
  • Always use a dry spoon to take out pickle from the jar.
